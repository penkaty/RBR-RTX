# Code of Conduct

RBR-RTX is a hobby project made for and with the Richard Burns Rally community. Everyone who takes part (issues,
pull requests, discussions, reports, stage and sun fixes) is expected to keep it a friendly place.

## Expected
- Be respectful and patient, with newcomers too. Not everyone knows RTX Remix, path tracing or modding.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person. Say what is wrong and, if you can, how to show it (a sweep, a
  screenshot, a stage and car).
- Accept that maintainers decide what goes into the project, and explain your view when you disagree.
- Respect other people's work: credit authors, follow licences, and do not post game files or anything you do not
  have the right to share.

## Not accepted
- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats, or attacks on someone's identity, background or skill level.
- Sexual or violent content, trolling, spam, or repeatedly derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Pirated games, cracked files or links to them.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who do not follow
this code. To report a problem, open a private report through GitHub (the "Report content" option on the comment or
issue) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies to the project's GitHub spaces and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
